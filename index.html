INKLANDS

Shape the valley. Guide the golden river. Wake the light.
PILOT

SHAPE  Drag with Raise and Lower to sculpt the land under your finger.

FLOW  The spring always pours. Its golden river finds its own way down, pools on the flats, and spills over lips. Carve and dam to guide it.

LIGHT  One shaft of light drifts across the valley. Bring the river to the pavilion terrace and fill its reflecting pool, and the valley is Lit. Groves you plant will spread toward water over time.
